## Topology Videos

Below you can find Youtube links for my topology lecture videos. The videos were used both for the
advanced undergraduate course *Math 431*, and for the graduate core course *Math 631*, even though
the undergraduate course might not cover all topics, while the graduate course might cover more.
The videos are based on my lecture notes, and the book section numbers refer to the book 
*Topology* by James Munkres (Second edition, Pearson Modern Classics, 2017).
